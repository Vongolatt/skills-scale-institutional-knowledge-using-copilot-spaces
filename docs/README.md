# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme’s project management guidance, from validating an idea through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The documents provide a shared reference for project teams and a starting point for new teammates.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme initiates and approves work by clarifying the business need, stakeholders, desired outcomes, success measures, and initial risks. Once approved, the team plans the work as prioritized, estimated increments with clear acceptance criteria, dependencies, and a Definition of Done. During execution, the team tracks work on a project board, uses small reviewed pull requests and CI/testing to maintain quality, and checks progress through regular ceremonies, metrics, and blocker escalation. Before release, the team confirms readiness, verifies deployment, and prepares rollback or mitigation steps. At the end of a sprint, release, or milestone, the team reflects on outcomes and turns lessons into owned, time-bound improvement actions.
